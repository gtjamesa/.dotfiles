# Dotfiles

Personal shell environment and desktop setup, plus the small automations that look after it.

## Language

### Recordings

**Clip**:
Any video OBS has saved into the recordings folder, whatever its name or location.
_Avoid_: Video, capture

**Recording**:
A clip from a full OBS recording session, named `YYYY-MM-DD hh-mm-ss.mp4` after its start time.
_Avoid_: VOD, session

**Replay**:
A clip saved from the OBS replay buffer, named `Replay YYYY-MM-DD hh-mm-ss.mp4`.
_Avoid_: Instant replay, highlight

**Raw clip**:
A Recording or Replay that still has its OBS-given name and sits loose in the recordings folder. Raw clips are disposable after 14 days.
_Avoid_: Unsorted clip, temp clip

**Kept clip**:
A clip that has been renamed or filed into a subfolder. Kept clips are never deleted automatically.
_Avoid_: Saved clip, archived clip
